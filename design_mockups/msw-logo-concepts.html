MySportsWeather: 5 logo directions

Rough concepts to react to, not finished artwork. Each one goes a different way on purpose.

1. Varsity Pennant

Old-school college bookstore: felt pennant, stitched border, script plus block letters. Warm and nostalgic, made for football Saturdays and Sundays.

2. Minimal Modern

App-store clean. One black circle (ball, or sun) sliced by wind streaks, lowercase wordmark, one orange accent. Works tiny as a favicon and on an app icon.

3. The Weather Map

Pure meteorologist. Isobars, a real station-model wind barb as the icon, and cold and warm fronts as the underline. Says "a forecaster made this" to anyone who knows a weather map.

4. Prime Time

Sportsbook and broadcast energy: dark background, heavy italic "MSW", neon gradient, a lightning bolt. Loud, built for bettors and for social posts.

5. Field Badge

National-park patch: a round emblem with a stadium under a rain cloud, curved type, earthy greens and mustard. Feels handmade and trustworthy, and makes good merch.
